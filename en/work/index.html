DELIVERY SYSTEM
From Development to Live Operations

Every stage owns the outcome.

Six core capabilities form one continuous development and operations cycle, rather than a set of isolated departmental tasks.

01

Game Product Development

Starting with target players and the core experience, we bring gameplay, systems, content, and technical implementation together into a complete product.

02

Release Planning and Reliable Delivery

We define goals, scope, and milestones for each version so priority content ships reliably on a clear schedule.

03

Cross-functional Development

Game design, art, engineering, QA, and live operations align around the same product outcome.

04

Quality Assurance and Risk Management

Reviews, testing, data, and player feedback help us protect experience quality and operational stability.

05

Process and Productivity Improvement

We streamline information flows and build reusable tools and practices so teams can collaborate with greater consistency and efficiency.

06

Ongoing Support for Overseas Releases

Feedback from players in Japan and other markets informs version updates, live events, and long-term operations.
