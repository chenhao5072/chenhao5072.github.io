01 / MOBILE GAME R&D
Our Focus

Starting with ideas and gameplay, we create natural, complete experiences for mobile.

We continually test, adjust, and refine the work so the core experience remains clear and engaging on mobile devices.

Every iteration returns to how players feel, improving controls, pacing, communication, and overall product quality.

03 / PROCESS
How We Develop

Every step leads back to the player experience.

01 / DEFINE

Define the Experience

We first define what players should feel, then decide how best to express it.

02 / PROTOTYPE

Prototype Early

We turn ideas into something the team can discuss and experience as early as possible.

03 / ITERATE

Iterate Continuously

We learn from real play, identify problems, and keep refining the details that matter.

04 / REFINE

Refine the Whole

Gameplay, pacing, and communication all work together in service of product quality.

Focused on Japan, creating for players around the world.
